Don't Chase Pending Payments. Let AI Handle the Follow-ups.

TapFee's AI Voice Agent helps businesses follow up with customers who have pending payments through automated phone calls. Instead of manually calling every customer, your AI agent can contact customers with unpaid fees, remind them about pending payments and guide them on the next steps.

Identify Unpaid Customers

TapFee identifies customers with pending payments based on their payment status.

Schedule AI Calls

The AI Voice Agent initiates follow-up calls according to your configured follow-up schedule.

Natural Voice Conversations

The agent informs customers about pending payments and answers supported questions.
